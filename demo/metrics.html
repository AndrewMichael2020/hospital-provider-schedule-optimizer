How to read the demo

Executive summary metrics

Definitions

What each number means

Shortage hours avoided

Estimated role-hours of uncovered demand removed by the optimized overflow plan compared with the original overflow process.

original shortage - optimized shortage
Coverage-risk hours avoided

Hours where at least one role still has an uncovered gap. Lower means fewer time blocks requiring operational escalation.

original risk hours - optimized risk hours
Funded capacity used

Share of the funded overflow bank consumed by the recommendation. Role banks remain separate for MD, NP, RN, and HCW.

used overflow hours / funded overflow hours
Expected overflow role-hours

Additional role coverage still needed over the 30-day synthetic case after the current optimized overflow plan is applied. This is not per day.

sum(max(required - core - overflow, 0))
Hours with a remaining gap

Count of facility-hours in the 30-day case where that role still has any remaining uncovered need.

count(hours where remaining gap > 0)
Recommendation status

A simple demo signal. Review appears when the selected parameters use all funded capacity but leave weak improvement.

ready unless constrained and weak

Method

How the optimizer avoids overflow gaps

1. Start with protected rotations

The engine expands the fixed MD, NP, RN, and HCW rotations into hourly role coverage by facility. These core shifts are treated as locked; the optimizer does not move or cancel them.

2. Simulate role-specific demand

The synthetic demand engine creates hourly required staffing by role. Normal demand sits below core coverage with slack. Moderate and surge scenarios add facility, evening, weekend, flu, heatwave, and ambulance-arrival pressure.

3. Measure the original gap

For each facility-hour and role, the engine computes the uncovered gap before optimized overflow.

gap = max(required role coverage - protected core coverage, 0)
4. Compare against original overflow

The original baseline mimics threshold-triggered call-outs. It can react to waiting-room or surge signals, but may be early, late, unavailable, or aimed at the wrong role or site.

5. Score candidate overflow shifts

Every available overflow shift is scored by how many uncovered role-hours it can cover, weighted by operational impact. MD, NP, RN, and HCW are scored separately so one role's bank does not silently solve another role's shortage.

score = covered role gap hours * role weight + pressure priority
6. Select the best feasible plan

The optimizer uses a greedy constrained selection: take the highest-value overflow shift that still covers enough gap hours, stays inside each role's funded bank, and does not double-book the same provider-hour.

7. Recompute what remains

After selected overflow is applied, the engine recomputes remaining uncovered role-hours and facility-hours with any post-optimization gap. Avoided shortage is the difference between original and optimized shortage.

avoided shortage = original overflow shortage - optimized overflow shortage
