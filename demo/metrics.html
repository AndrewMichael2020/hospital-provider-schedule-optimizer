How to read the demo

Executive summary metrics

Definitions

What each number means

Shortage hours avoided

Estimated role-hours of uncovered demand removed by the optimized overflow plan compared with the original overflow process.

original shortage - optimized shortage
Coverage-risk hours avoided

Hours where at least one role still has an uncovered gap. Lower means fewer time blocks requiring operational escalation.

original risk hours - optimized risk hours
Funded capacity used

Share of the funded overflow bank consumed by the recommendation. Role banks remain separate for MD, NP, RN, and HCW.

used overflow hours / funded overflow hours
Uncovered role-hours

Total remaining demand over the 30-day synthetic case after optimized overflow is applied. This is not per day.

sum(max(required - core - overflow, 0))
Hours with a remaining gap

Count of facility-hours in the 30-day case where that role still has any remaining uncovered need.

count(hours where remaining gap > 0)
Recommendation status

A simple demo signal. Review appears when the selected parameters use all funded capacity but leave weak improvement.

ready unless constrained and weak
